Payments, Al Fardan Exchange

AANI local and cross-border integration

AANI is the UAE's instant payment platform. I work on the integration that lets customers send money instantly within the UAE and abroad, through several partner banks, on one distributed system.

What I built

  • Event-driven services on Kafka, so each stage of a payment can scale and recover on its own.
  • ISO 20022 message handling, including pacs.008 credit transfers and camt statements.
  • Partner-bank connectivity, where each bank has its own rules but the core flow stays the same.
  • Lifecycle tracking and reconciliation, so every transfer has a status and a matching ledger entry.

Why it matters

In payments, a lost or duplicated message is real money. The design favours idempotent processing, clear status transitions and an audit trail that operations teams can actually use.

Talend to Spring Batch migration

A migration of more than 100 production batch jobs from Talend to Spring Batch, keeping schedules and downstream consumers working throughout.

What I did

  • Rebuilt each Talend job as a Spring Batch job with readers, processors and writers in plain Java.
  • Kept scheduling and dependencies in AutoSys, so operations didn't have to change how they run things.
  • Checked outputs job by job against the old pipeline before switching over.

Result

The jobs now live in the same codebase, tooling and review process as the rest of the Java services, which makes them easier to test, version and change.
