Payments, Al Fardan Exchange

AANI–UPI instant payments for the India–UAE corridor

A greenfield integration between the UAE's AANI instant-payments platform and India's UPI, through the UPI IRP channel. It lets customers send and receive remittances between the two countries in real time, in line with new regulatory requirements.

What I built

  • The integration architecture, co-designed with the team lead.
  • ISO 20022 message handlers for pacs.008, camt.052, camt.053 and camt.054.
  • End-to-end message pipelines for parsing, validation, transformation and persistence, with Kafka for event-driven processing.
  • An XML-to-JSON mapping framework that keeps data intact across remittance and reconciliation flows.
  • Java 21 / Spring Boot services secured with Spring Security and JWT, running in Docker on AWS with CloudWatch.
  • Audit trails for every parsed and unparsed message, for regulatory traceability.

Why it matters

In payments, a lost or duplicated message is real money. The design favours clear status transitions, idempotent processing and an audit trail that operations teams can use to trace any transfer end to end.

My role

Co-architect and hands-on developer. I also mentor the team's two engineers on system design and review their code.
